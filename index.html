Mazehold Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains how the Android game Mazehold (the "game"), published by BlueRampartGames ("we", "us"), handles information.

Summary

Mazehold does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The game works fully offline.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal data. It has no user accounts.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels completed, star ratings, best scores, in-game currency balance, and sound setting) in a small file stored locally on your device. This file is not sent anywhere and never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Internet access, ads, and analytics

The game does not request internet access. It contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party tracking code.

Third parties

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any. Distribution of the game through Google Play is subject to Google's own privacy practices, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle data (for example, in-app purchases), we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before releasing that version.
